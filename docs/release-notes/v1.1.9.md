## What's changed

- The quota card in the chat panel now has a Refresh button, so a current number no longer means opening Settings.
- ChatGPT reset credits are shown, and can be spent from the card — guarded so a credit is only offered when the weekly quota is below 5%, with a confirmation before it is used.
- Fixes a bug where refreshing an account made the quota card disappear until the session was switched.
- Stops reading a field the provider never sends, which is why reset credits had never appeared.
